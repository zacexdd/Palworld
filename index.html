What Is Palworld Mobile?

Palworld Mobile is the official mobile version of Palworld, the smash-hit creature-collection survival game by Pocketpair. The mobile adaptation is being developed by Krafton's PUBG Studios under a formal licensing agreement with Pocketpair.

Unlike most mobile ports, Krafton is not simply scaling down the PC version. The studio has built a new skill-based strategic battle system designed specifically for touch controls, while keeping the core loop of Pal capturing, base building, crafting, and survival. Social features are expanded too, including Pal breeding in a shared Plaza and cooperative or competitive boss battles with other players.

The game was first shown publicly at G-STAR 2025 in South Korea. Krafton has confirmed the game will not use a live-service monetisation model, a significant commitment for a mobile title of this scale.

Palworld Mobile vs Palworld Online: Two Different Games

In August 2026, a second Palworld mobile title was announced. Here is how the two differ so you know exactly what you are looking for:

  • Palworld Mobile by Krafton: A faithful mobile adaptation of the original survival game. Developed by PUBG Studios. Closed alpha completed in Japan and South Korea. No global release date yet.
  • Palworld Online by Garena: A brand new mobile MMO with an original story, seamless open world, and large-scale multiplayer. Licensed by Pocketpair but developed entirely by Garena. Announced August 3, 2026 for iOS and Android later in 2026.

Both are officially licensed from Pocketpair. They are completely separate games made by different studios. See Palworld Online for full details on the Garena title.
