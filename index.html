Play Palworld on your phone with StarDesk

Palworld doesn't have an installable mobile app today — KRAFTON's official Palworld Mobile is still in development and only open to pre-registration. What you can do right now is stream your own PC copy of Palworld to your Android phone or iPhone using StarDesk, a free remote-play app. This guide walks through buying the game, installing StarDesk on both devices, and getting a smooth stream.

Buy Palworld on Steam → Download StarDesk (official) →

Setup, step by step

Do this once. After the first pairing, starting a session takes seconds.

Buy and install Palworld on your PC

StarDesk streams your own copy of the game — it doesn't include Palworld itself. Buy it on Steam and install it on the Windows PC you'll be streaming from.

Palworld on Steam →

Install StarDesk on that same PC

Download StarDesk from its official site and sign in (it uses Google sign-in to register your devices). This PC becomes the "host."

StarDesk download (official) →

Install StarDesk on your phone

Get the StarDesk app from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone) and sign in with the same account you used on the PC — devices pair automatically once you're signed into both.

Get app links from StarDesk's site →

Launch Palworld, then connect from your phone

Start Palworld on your PC (or launch it remotely through StarDesk if it supports quick-launch). Open StarDesk on your phone, select your PC from your device list, and the stream begins.

Set up controls

Use on-screen touch controls, or pair a Bluetooth controller to your phone first for a more console-like feel — Palworld's combat and building are easier with a controller.

Getting a good connection

🏠 Same Wi‑Fi first

Start on the same network as your PC before trying remote connections over the internet — it's the easiest way to confirm everything works.

📡 5GHz over 2.4GHz

If your router supports both, use 5GHz on your phone for less interference and higher throughput.

🎮 Bluetooth controller

Most Bluetooth controllers pair directly with Android and iOS and make combat noticeably easier.

🔋 Keep the PC awake

Disable sleep mode on the host PC so StarDesk can always reach it.

Note: this streams your own licensed PC copy of Palworld. It doesn't substitute for KRAFTON's upcoming official mobile release, which is a separate, still-unreleased game.
