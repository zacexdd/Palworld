What Is Palworld Online?

Palworld Online is an officially licensed mobile MMORPG set in the Palworld universe, developed and published by Garena in close partnership with Pocketpair — the original creators of Palworld. It is built specifically for Android and iPhone, not a port of the PC game.

Rather than adapting the existing game, Palworld Online features an original story and exclusive side stories created just for this title. Players explore a vast, seamless open world, capture and raise Pals, build shared bases with friends, and take on both cooperative PvE content and competitive PvP battles with players worldwide.

The interface has been redesigned from the ground up for mobile, with touch-friendly controls and optional one-tap actions for building, Pal deployment, and base management.

Palworld Online vs Palworld Mobile: What's the Difference?

There are currently two separate licensed Palworld mobile games in development, which is causing confusion online:

  • Palworld Online — Developed by Garena. A mobile MMORPG with an original story, seamless open world, shared bases, PvE and PvP. Announced August 3, 2026. No gameplay footage released yet.
  • Palworld Mobile — Developed by Krafton (PUBG publisher). A faithful reinterpretation of the original Palworld survival gameplay for mobile. Closed alpha already completed in Japan and South Korea in late 2025.

Both are officially licensed by Pocketpair, but they are made by different studios and offer different experiences. This page covers Palworld Online by Garena.

Palworld Online Gameplay Features

Garena has outlined the core pillars of Palworld Online's gameplay. Here is what has been officially confirmed so far:

🌍 Seamless Open World

A vast, interconnected world to explore freely, discover hidden treasures, uncover lore, and venture into new regions with your Pals.

🐾 Pal Capture & Raising

Catch Pals across the open world, including rare variants. Raise them, manage them, and deploy them for base work and combat.

🏠 Shared Base Building

Build and expand communal settlements with friends. Pals automate production, defend your base, and manage resources for your group.

Take on challenging cooperative PvE content and world bosses with your party, or compete against other players in PvP battles.
