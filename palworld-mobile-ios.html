How to Play Palworld on iPhone Right Now

The official Palworld Mobile download for iPhone is not available yet. You can already play the full PC version of Palworld on your iPhone right now through remote streaming. No unofficial downloads are needed and your real save file is used.

  • Steam Link: Valve's free Steam Link app is available on the App Store. Stream your Palworld PC copy to your iPhone over Wi-Fi. Both devices need to be on the same network. A controller is recommended but touch controls work too.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming: Available on iPhone through the Xbox Game Pass app. Requires Game Pass Ultimate. Streams Palworld from Microsoft's servers, no PC needed. Works on any iPhone with a solid internet connection.
  • NVIDIA GeForce Now via browser: iPhone users can access GeForce Now through the Safari browser. Stream the PC version of Palworld if you own it on Steam. Free tier available with wait times, paid tiers for priority access.
  • PlayStation Remote Play: If you own Palworld on PS5, stream it to your iPhone using the PlayStation Remote Play app, available free on the App Store.

All of these use the full real Palworld game, require no unofficial downloads, and are completely safe for your device.
