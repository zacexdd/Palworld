Palworld Mobile Online: New MMO Announced

Garena and Pocketpair just announced Palworld Online, a new Palworld mobile MMO separate from the existing Krafton mobile game. Here's everything confirmed so far.

Last updated: August 04, 2026

Download on Mobile

Is Palworld Coming to Mobile as an MMO?

Yes. On August 3, 2026, Pocketpair and mobile publisher Garena announced Palworld Online, a mobile MMO built specifically for iOS and Android. This is a separate title from the existing Krafton-published Palworld mobile app, and it's the biggest expansion of the Palworld mobile space to date.

Garena, the Singapore-based publisher behind mobile versions of League of Legends, Path of Exile, and EA Sports FC, is developing and publishing the title under official license from Pocketpair.

Quick answer: Palworld Online is a new, standalone Palworld mobile MMO developed by Garena. It launches sometime in 2026 on iOS and Android, with online co-op, PvE, base building, and Pal capturing — separate from the main PC/console game and separate from the earlier Krafton mobile title.

Confirmed Features

  • Seamless open world: One connected map rather than instanced zones, built around exploration and Pal collecting.
  • New story and lore: Original narrative content and side stories not found in the main Palworld game.
  • Online co-op and PvE: Players can group up to take on bosses and challenges together.
  • PvP: Competitive modes are planned alongside cooperative content.
  • Shared base building: Players can build and grow settlements together, with Pals automating production and defense.
  • Mobile-first controls: A touch-optimized interface with simplified one-tap options, aimed at working across a wide range of phone hardware.

What's Still Unknown

  • Release date: Only "sometime in 2026" has been confirmed.
  • New Pals or map regions: Not detailed yet.
  • Monetization: No word on microtransactions — notable since the core Palworld game has none.
  • Pre-registration: Not yet open on either app store.
